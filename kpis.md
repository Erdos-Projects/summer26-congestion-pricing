# KPIs

Current KPI definitions for the NYC CBD congestion-pricing project. These
metrics support an inference-focused descriptive analysis: they track burden,
exposure, volume change, cost movement, and robustness. They should be described
as associated with post-policy trip patterns unless a specific model supports a
stronger claim.

## KPI Table

| KPI | Definition | Service scope | Current status | Source / output | Interpretation caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zone disruption score (`DS_z`) | Mean `cbd_congestion_fee / round(passenger_cost_pretip - cbd_congestion_fee, 2)` among qualifying charged trips, by zone and direction. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Implemented symmetrically for both services: burden ranking, floor robustness, and heterogeneity exported under `results/burden_analysis/`. | `results/burden_analysis/tables/{yellow,hvfhv}_top_dsz_zone_directions.csv`; `data/processed/disruption_score/{yellow,hvfhv}_zone_disruption_score.csv`; `docs/{yellow,hvfhv}_data_audit.md`; `docs/burden_analysis_and_modeling_plan.md` | Burden ranking, not causal proof. Same-year cost/fare controls are mechanically close to the denominator and should not be used as ordinary controls. |
| Relative CBD burden | `cbd_congestion_fee / passenger_cost_pretip` when pre-tip cost is positive. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Available in standardized data; used as reference/sensitivity, not the primary DS_z metric. | `docs/yellow_dropped_and_engineered_features.md`; `docs/hvfhv_dropped_and_engineered_features.md` | Closely related to DS_z. Do not include both as independent explanatory features unless explicitly labeled as a sensitivity check. |
| Trip volume change | Percent change in trip counts from Feb-Jun 2024 to Feb-Jun 2025 by zone and direction, where the baseline is defined. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Implemented in disruption-score outputs. Yellow volume excludes Flex for the clean policy-signal population. | `data/processed/disruption_score/hvfhv_ds_z_vs_volume_change.csv`; `data/processed/disruption_score/yellow_ds_z_vs_volume_change.csv` | Outcome variable. Do not use `pct_volume_change`, `delta_volume`, or `n_2025` as predictors of themselves. |
| Charged-trip share / geographic exposure | Share of trips charged in 2025, or pre-policy geography-based exposure such as CRZ pickup/dropoff share for Model 2. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | 2025 charged-trip summaries exist. Yellow `charged_geo` validation exists. HVFHV Model 2 monthly panel and 2024 geography-based exposure are available. | `data/processed/disruption_score/yellow_charged_geo_validation.csv`; `data/processed/disruption_score/hvfhv_monthly_panel.csv`; `data/processed/disruption_score/hvfhv_model2_exposure_validation.csv`; `docs/burden_analysis_and_modeling_plan.md`; `docs/hvfhv_dropped_and_engineered_features.md` | Observed `charged_cbd_flag` is post-policy. For DiD, use a geography rule that can be computed in 2024. HVFHV Model 2 is estimated under assumptions and has a placebo warning. |
| Average pre-tip passenger cost | Mean or median `passenger_cost_pretip`, by service, month, zone, or direction. Yellow uses `total_amount - tip_amount`; HVFHV reconstructs cost from TLC components. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Used in audits and behavioral-shift outputs. | `docs/yellow_data_audit.md`; `docs/hvfhv_data_audit.md`; `data/processed/disruption_score/hvfhv_behavioral_shift.csv`; `data/processed/disruption_score/yellow_behavioral_shift.csv` | Cost movement is descriptive. HVFHV cost may miss platform discounts/refunds/credits; Yellow Flex Fare is a separate regime and should not be pooled into primary financial analysis. |
| Distance/time controls | Pre-policy trip-shape measures such as 2024 average distance; time fields for EDA and seasonality. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Used or recommended as controls/sensitivities in Model 1 and Model 2. Yellow distance aggregates appear in disruption-score outputs; HVFHV distance controls come from 2024 trip-level aggregates in the Model 1 feature table when used. | `docs/burden_analysis_and_modeling_plan.md`; `docs/yellow_dropped_and_engineered_features.md`; `docs/hvfhv_dropped_and_engineered_features.md`; `data/processed/disruption_score/yellow_behavioral_shift.csv` (Yellow distance fields) | Use pre-policy values only. Treat duration cautiously because congestion and post-policy traffic conditions can affect it. |
| Robustness metrics | DS_z floor sensitivity, rank stability, top-zone overlap, Pearson/Spearman correlations, Manhattan-only correlation checks, and no-June pretrend/placebo diagnostics. | Yellow Taxi, HVFHV | Implemented symmetrically for both services: floor rank-stability + top-zone overlap, Model 1 correlations/quartiles/specs, Model 2 estimates/diagnostics, and the no-June 2023-vs-2024 placebo. | `results/burden_analysis/tables/{yellow,hvfhv}_dsz_floor_rank_stability.csv`, `{yellow,hvfhv}_dsz_floor_top_overlap.csv`; `results/modeling/tables/{yellow,hvfhv}_model1_correlations.csv`, `{yellow,hvfhv}_model2_diagnostics.csv`, `{yellow,hvfhv}_model2_exposure_validation.csv` | Robustness summaries test stability of associations and rankings. Both services' placebos are negative and similar in magnitude to Model 2, which weakens causal interpretation. |
